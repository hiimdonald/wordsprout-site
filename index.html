How a week goes

Built for the weekly spelling list

Add the words from school in a minute. Your child practices a few minutes a day. You see who’s ready before test day.

  1. Monday

    Add this week’s list

    Type the words, paste them from the teacher’s email, or snap a photo of the paper list.

  2. Every day

    Practice in one go

    A few quick games, a Spelling check with no hints, then a big cheer. Every word is read aloud.

  3. Test day

    Know they’re ready

    Every word shows Ready, Almost or Tricky, so you know exactly what to review the night before.

For parents helping one or more kids. Your phone opens to a parent home. Each child gets their own lists, garden and progress.

Questions parents ask

Does it work with my child’s school list?

Yes. WordSprout is built around whatever list comes home: type it, paste it, or snap a photo of the paper. Any words work, from kindergarten sight words to 5th-grade spelling.

Do I need to record every word?

No. The device voice reads any word you haven’t recorded. Record your own voice for words you want to say your way.

Is my child’s data private?

WordSprout has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos you add for a child stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Two parents want to use it. How?

Sign in to the same WordSprout account on both phones. Lists, recordings and progress stay in sync.
