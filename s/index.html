How to add it

  1. Get WordSprout from the App Store. It’s free.
  2. Open WordSprout and tap + to make a new list.
  3. Choose Use a code and enter the code, or scan the card’s QR code there.
  4. The list, with its words and recordings, is added to your app. Your child’s practice stays private to your family.
